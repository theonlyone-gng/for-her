A letter for Piya

My sweetest Piya,

Happy 18 months, my love. Sometimes I sit and think about how one person can become so important to you that even the smallest things remind you of them. Somehow, that person became you. You became the name I look forward to seeing, the voice I want to hear, the person I want to tell about my day, and the one I wish I could pull close whenever the distance feels too big.

I wish I could give you this website in person, watch your face while you discover every little part, hold your hand, and tell you all of this while looking straight into your eyes. For now, I wanted to make you a small place on the internet that belongs only to us — a place where you can come back whenever you need a reminder of how deeply you are loved.

You are special to me in ways I don't always know how to explain. It's not only the beautiful things about you. It's your little habits, your laugh, your reactions, the way you can make an ordinary day feel different just by being in it. It's the way I can miss you even right after we say goodbye. It's how a future that once felt far away starts to feel real when I imagine you standing beside me.

I know I am not perfect. Sometimes I don't find the right words, sometimes I get things wrong, and sometimes I wish I could express everything inside my heart better than I do. But I want to keep learning how to love you well: to listen to you, understand you, make you feel appreciated, and give you reasons to feel safe with me. You deserve love that shows up in the little things, not only in beautiful words on an anniversary.

Thank you for being my Piya. Thank you for every call, every laugh, every sweet moment, every memory we have made from miles apart. Thank you for being part of my life and for giving me someone so wonderful to dream about. If I could choose again, knowing all the waiting and all the miles, I would still want the chance to know you and love you.

One day, I hope this distance will be a story we tell while sitting next to each other. No phone between us, no screen to kiss goodnight through, no counting how long until the next time. Just us, sharing the same room, the same quiet moments, and all the ordinary days that I know will feel extraordinary because they are with you.

For now, please remember this: you matter to me. Your feelings matter. Your dreams matter. Your happiness matters. You are not just a date on my calendar or a message on my phone. You are someone I carry with me through my day, someone I make plans with in my heart, and someone I am grateful to call mine.

I love you, Piya. More than this page could ever fit, more than one anniversary message could ever explain, and in a thousand little ways I hope I get to show you for a very, very long time.

Always your Moemen ♡
